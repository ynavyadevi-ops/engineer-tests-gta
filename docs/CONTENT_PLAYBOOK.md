# Content playbook

## Editorial pillars (separate from formats)

1. **Systems & Myth Tests**
2. **Map, World & Hidden Details**
3. **Missions & Useful Discoveries**

GTA IV/V/San Andreas comparison is a *lens* across the pillars, not a fourth format.

## Publishing lanes

| Lane | Role |
|---|---|
| YouTube long-form | Full investigation, comparison or useful guide |
| YouTube Shorts | One fast visual test or payoff |
| Instagram Reels | A platform-adapted edit of suitable original capture, with its own spoken and on-screen context and payoff. Never "repost this Short". **Output format only:** not a data source, performance estimate or YouTube proxy |

Lanes attach to **original capture sessions** with reusable capture IDs.

## 30-day plan

- **Inputs:** launch or start date (never hardcoded), editing hours, solo creator time, target posting window, languages, content preferences, available platform/build (never assume access), spoiler policy, mechanic verification.
- **Defaults:** time zone `Asia/Kolkata`; weekly budget 10–12 h (editable); starter mix **4 long-form / 12 Shorts / 8 Reels** across four editorial weeks; Days 29–30 for review and buffer.
- Every day 1–30 has a filming, editing, publishing, reviewing, or genuine rest/buffer action. Targets give way to constraints. The plan never implies 30 unique shoots and never defaults to daily long-form.
- A lean **Days 1–3 early-launch queue** comes first.
- Show planned vs actual counts per platform, show overruns, and explain omitted items.
- **Editing:** seeded hypothesis templates, own ideas, edit, pin, rebuild unlocked slots, swap, skip/replace, copy a brief. Pinned slots survive a rebuild.
- **Export:** original brief and calendar text only. No bulk YouTube metadata or metrics.

## Brief template (every field required)

Working title + alternate · pillar · format · viewer question · original angle · first frame · speakable cold open · shot checklist · test/control or guide verification · **two genuinely different thumbnail hypotheses** (long-form) or cover/first-frame design (vertical) · payoff beat · rough duration · spoiler level · time/cost · feasible fallback if unverified · example reference when available · own-channel success measure and review time.

For a Reel, state how framing, pacing and context change.

## Capture protocol rules

- A test must be filmable with footage the creator can capture.
- Use an unobstructed control, the same setup (vehicle, time of day) where feasible, and keep failed attempts.
- Never fabricate multiplayer activities, GTA VI mechanics, locations or results. A "GTA VI adaptation" is a capture suggestion with status *unknown* or *verified in own gameplay*.
- Reject generic prompts ("Top 10 Secrets") unless evidence and a visual test justify the specific promise.

## Example output shape (illustrative, not a research finding)

- **Pillar:** Systems & Myth Tests.
- **Question:** Does the same blocked lane trigger different reactions in two districts?
- **Capture:** Only if vehicle placement and NPC traffic allow the test in your own GTA VI build. Record an unobstructed control, then repeat with the same vehicle and time of day where feasible. Keep failed attempts. If the test is not feasible, pivot to a reproducible comparison you can actually capture.
- **Long-form:** Open on two genuinely different reactions if you recorded them. Then show the control, the repeat attempts, and what the footage does and doesn't prove. Thumbnail variants: a decisive reaction close-up vs a clean two-district split, both from your own capture.
- **Short:** One obstruction, one visibly surprising response, one payoff.
- **Reel:** Reframe the wider scene vertically and add a short plain-language setup for viewers who haven't seen the long video.
- **Evidence:** Link verified catalog examples if permitted. Never say "10M views" unless the app pulled that count and shows its source and fetch date.
- **Review:** Compare against your own format-appropriate checkpoint, record the effort, and decide whether to repeat the test or move to a different mechanic.

## Weekly review loop (Phase 10)

- **Ledger:** original title and thumbnail variants, actual publish time, format, capture and edit time, hypothesis, who changed which creative element and when, and outcome notes.
- Optional manual Studio metrics at **24 h and day 7**, left blank if unavailable.
- Mark each release **promising / retest / retire** with a short rationale. Distinguish a variant test from co-varying changes.
- Carry 2–3 observations forward and regenerate next week or next month without overwriting locked work.
- Reel production and publishing status are tracked as operations notes only. No Instagram metrics.
